This week

  • Two-nil to start. Estêvão's opener after twelve minutes, Lavia's first full ninety in a year, and West Ham managed one real spell all afternoon. The calmest opening day in memory.
  • Lacroix is through the door. £52m from Palace, medical done Tuesday — the back line finally has a right-footed organiser.
  • Villa's set-piece brain poached. Austin MacPhee's release clause triggered midweek. Chelsea scored eight from corners all last season; this is the quiet fix.
  • Chalobah edging toward Como. Third bid on the table. Sad, sensible, probably done by Friday.

Today

No Chelsea, and honestly a decent day to log off — the only game with teeth is at St James' Park, the first look at how Liverpool line up without Konaté. Watch the opening twenty; if Newcastle press like last season, stay for the rest.
